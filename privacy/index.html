Subs

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Subs is made by Joan Morales. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps what you enter in a database on your device:

  • Subscriptions: name, price, billing cycle, next renewal date, category, trial dates, reminder choices, and a history of price changes.
  • Cards: a label you choose, the last four digits if you add them, and a color. The app does not ask for a full card number, an expiration date, or a security code.
  • Settings: appearance, currency, and notification preferences.

The app also reads the language and region of the device to choose the interface language and a default currency. That choice stays on the device.

None of this is sent to Joan Morales or to a server run for Subs.

What the app does not do

There is no account, no sign-in, and no sync. The app does not include advertising or analytics, and it does not sell information.

Apple and Google distribute the app. Their own policies cover the store account you use to download it.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules them on your device. A Subs server does not send them.

Export

Settings can export your subscriptions to a CSV file. The app creates that file on your device and opens the system share sheet. You decide whether to send it anywhere.

Deleting information

You can delete a subscription or a card inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its database from the device.

Children

Subs is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
